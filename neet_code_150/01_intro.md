# NeetCode 150 – Learn Data Structures & Algorithms with Python

## Introduction

Welcome to NeetCode 150!

This repository is a collection of 150 carefully selected coding problems covering the most important concepts and patterns in **Data Structures and Algorithms (DSA)**.

The goal of this repository is to help students and developers build strong problem-solving skills, understand DSA concepts through practical problems, and prepare for technical coding interviews.

You don't need to be an expert programmer to start. The problems gradually introduce different concepts, starting with easier questions and progressing towards more challenging ones.

## What Will You Learn?

By working through these 150 problems, you will gain practical experience with:

- **Arrays & Hashing:** Working with arrays, dictionaries, sets, and efficient lookups.
- **Two Pointers:** Solving problems using two positions to reduce unnecessary operations.
- **Sliding Window:** Efficiently solving problems involving continuous sections of arrays and strings.
- **Stack:** Understanding LIFO and its applications.
- **Binary Search:** Searching efficiently in sorted data and learning variations of binary search.
- **Linked Lists:** Understanding nodes, pointers, and linked list operations.
- **Trees & Binary Search Trees:** Learning tree traversal, recursion, and hierarchical problem-solving.
- **Tries:** Working with prefix trees and efficient string searches.
- **Heaps & Priority Queues:** Managing data based on priority.
- **Backtracking:** Solving problems by exploring different possible combinations and paths.
- **Graphs:** Understanding graph traversal, BFS, DFS, shortest paths, and connected components.
- **Dynamic Programming:** Breaking complex problems into smaller overlapping subproblems.
- **Greedy Algorithms:** Making locally optimal decisions to solve larger problems.
- **Intervals:** Working with overlapping ranges, sorting, and merging.
- **Math & Geometry:** Applying mathematical concepts to programming problems.
- **Bit Manipulation:** Working directly with binary representations and bitwise operations.

## What Will You Gain?

After completing all 150 problems, you should have:

- A solid foundation in commonly used data structures and algorithms.
- An understanding of important problem-solving patterns.
- The ability to recognise patterns in unfamiliar coding problems.
- Better logical thinking and analytical skills.
- Experience analysing time and space complexity.
- More confidence when solving LeetCode problems.
- A stronger foundation for technical coding interviews.

## Who Is This For?

This repository is suitable for:

- Students starting their DSA journey.
- Beginners who want to improve their programming problem-solving skills.
- Developers preparing for technical interviews.
- Anyone who wants to practise LeetCode systematically.

**A small reminder:** The objective is not simply to complete 150 questions. It is to understand the underlying concepts and learn how to apply them to problems you haven't seen before.

Take your time, attempt each problem, understand the solution, and keep practising.
